# Social Media Post — ORK 3.5.2 "Mask"

---

The Amtgard ORK development team is pleased to announce that **ORK 3.5.2 "Mask" is live!** This one is all about making your profile feel like *yours* and telling your Amtgard story — plus a stack of upgrades for officers and a faster site all around.

**Tell your Amtgard Story**
Introducing **Design My Profile**: a markdown bio with Quick Add snippets, custom colors and hero gradients, a flexible name builder with title pickers, font choices (including Lexend for dyslexia), pronunciation guides, and privacy controls for your mundane name. Knights can pick how their belts display, too. Add your most important moments, first wins, awards, achievements, and other moments worth remembering to a personal **Milestones timeline** on your About tab.

**Boost the recs you love**
See an award recommendation you agree with? Hit the **+** to second it — optionally with a few words on why. You can also edit your own reason text on any recommendation you filed.

**Custom Title Aliases**
Honoring someone as Mentor, Archon, or Jarl in place of a standard title? Monarchies can now link those custom titles to the underlying award so they show up correctly in the Beltline Explorer and other reports.

**Faster everywhere**
Player profiles paint instantly and lazy-load heavier sections; big kingdom rosters render in year buckets; performance indexes and cache wins across attendance, awards, units, and search.

**Better tools for officers**
Park-level player merge for duplicate cleanup, expanded audit-log coverage, scoped Park/Kingdom Admin grants that stay scoped, and an inactive-kingdoms/parks cleanup view.

**A safer, more polished site**
Content guardrails on profile text, Open Sans typography site-wide, dark-mode contrast fixes, and dozens of smaller polish items.

Log in and try it out — start with the **Design My Profile** button on your own profile. 🎭
