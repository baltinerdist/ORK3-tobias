# Outside review 2: knot-match after the V12/V13 TL/top-L stall (2026-11-08)

Everything here was measured with the V13 tool (`knot-v13-tool`, 5e1cb9d20) and engine (`knot-v13-engine`), read-only, on the joint-page best `km/v13dev/e2/best.json` ("e2"). Every script and output is in `km/expt2/`. `lib.cjs` (my rsvg + `KM.regionScore` path) reproduces the tool's numbers exactly: TL 0.8430, top-L 0.8395, left 0.8558, right 0.8538, cap 0.8018 (`chk.cjs`). The tool's own `scoreCandidate` re-checks every candidate through `gate.cjs`.

## Headline

1. **The gap is real, and the optimiser's parameterisation is what limits it, not the engine.**
   - I ran a plain coordinate descent over per-knot normal offsets of the tile centrelines: one knot every 0.15 band, raised-cosine blending, ports pinned. The objective was the 4-phase jitter mean.
   - On the joint page it took **TL 0.8429 → 0.8665 and top-L 0.8395 → 0.8677**. The corner run took 2,011 renders and about 5 minutes on one worker; the end run took 1,083 renders and about 3 minutes.
   - The sides, cfg and every other parameter were unchanged. The joint score went from 0.8433 to **0.8611**.
   - **All three goal regions are now over 0.85 on one page with one engine:** sides 0.856/0.854, TL 0.867, top-L 0.868.
   - Candidate: `km/expt2/fit-both.json`.
2. **The gain is not pixel luck.** It holds at sub-pixel phases the search never saw, and at 2× resolution (tables in Q1 and Q2).
3. **The incumbent's nominal numbers are the luckiest sub-pixel phase.** For e2, the nominal TL, top-L and left scores are each the *maximum* of 16 quarter-pixel page shifts. The phase mean is about 0.018–0.020 lower. The CMA-ES and polish work of V12 and V13 was partly fitting the pixel grid, which explains its "noise floor".
4. **Scoring at higher resolution or with sub-pixel alignment does not help us; it raises the ceiling.**
   - At 2× the mirror ceiling rises to 0.94/0.95, but e2 gains only +0.005. A sub-pixel alignment search gives us nothing, because we already sit at the best phase, while the ceiling gains about 0.035.
   - The 1× ceiling (0.90/0.92) is held down by thin-ink aliasing. It is not a hard limit.

What this shows:
- **The sub-pixel sensitivity is about ±0.012 sd at 1× for us and ±0.025 for the mirror.** V13's "0.797–0.836 swing" is this effect.
- **For e2, nominal equals the 16-phase max in all three regions.** The selection process tuned the page to the pixel grid, so the reported numbers carry a winner's-curse bonus of about +0.02. Against the phase mean, e2 was about 0.025–0.03 short of 0.85, not 0.007–0.011. That shortfall is real geometry: at 2× it does not shrink, and the mirror's 2× score shows that Ash agrees with himself to 0.93–0.95.
- **A metric change would not have rescued e2, and the owner would have to approve one** (each option changes what 0.85 means):
  - sub-pixel alignment: e2 unchanged, ceiling +0.035;
  - 2×: e2 +0.005, ceiling +0.035;
  - phase mean: e2 −0.02.
- **Better search objective** (no metric change): optimise the phase mean (`--refJitter 0.5`, 4 phases), as `fit.cjs` does. Check the reported nominal afterwards. The fitted tiles are not at the phase max (TL max 0.874 > nominal 0.867), so they are not tuned to the pixel grid.

## Q2: best achievable within the current representation

`fit.cjs <best> <end|corner> <out> [h] [deltas] [sweeps]` searches only one tile's centreline paths. The end tile has the wrap and hairpin, 194 + 176 points. The corner tile has four polylines of 140–315 points.
- **Parameters:** the normal displacement at knots every h band of arc length, blended by a raised-cosine partition of unity. The first and last knot stay at 0, so the ports do not move.
- **Search:** coordinate descent, trying ±δ for each knot, with δ = 0.03 → 0.015 → 0.008 band (2.2 → 0.6 px) and up to 4 sweeps per δ.
- **Objective:** the target region's metric-v2 score, averaged over Ash at 4 half-pixel phases.
- **No gate during the search;** the result is gated afterwards.

## Q3: a smarter search

- **The per-point coordinate descent above *is* the smarter search.**
  - Rate: +0.024 (TL) and +0.028 (top-L) in 3–5 minutes on one worker while the machine was loaded (load average about 11, with chain19 running). That is roughly **+0.3 per hour while in the basin**. In practice it saturates after about 2,000 renders.
  - Compare: V12 + V13 gained < 0.01 over two full cycles of rounds.
- **Finite-difference "gradients":** a central-difference estimate on 100 knots costs 200 renders per step, which is the same as one coordinate sweep, and the landscape is piecewise flat at small δ (pixel quantisation). Coordinate descent with δ ≥ 0.008 band (about 0.6 px) is the right shape. A gradient method only pays off with a coverage/SDF-smoothed objective, so I don't recommend it now.
- **Nearest-control-point descent on the losing pixels:** a cheaper ordering of the same search (visit the knots near the error mass first). It would roughly halve the renders. Optional.

## Q5: structural findings

1. **Winner's curse on sub-pixel phase.**
   - Searching the nominal score selects phase-lucky designs: e2's nominal is the 16-phase max everywhere. That is the noise-floor stall V13 described.
   - **Change:** search on the jitter mean (as fit.cjs does), and accept a new best on that objective only. Keep reporting nominal as the metric.
   - **Side benefit:** for fit-both, the nominal sits in the middle of the phase range rather than at the top.
2. **Classifier AA bias** is real but uniform (Q4b).
3. **Region boxes** are fine. The TL "legs" are mostly the corner tile's own 2-band arms, so tile fitting fixes them: legs 0.813 → 0.849. Beyond 2 band the braid is global. If TL needs more margin, the lever is a longer-arm corner tile (arm 3), not braid parameters.
4. **The remaining loss is width, not position.**
   - After the fit, the residual is dominated by thin yellow ink↔colour inner-edge offsets inside every bar (error maps).
   - That is ink-ring and fill-width variation along each strand. The engine has only global thickness plus a low-order `fillField`.
   - A per-knot fill-width offset in the same coordinate descent is the next lever after centrelines. I did not run it.
5. **The raster path is not a source of error.** rsvg and `--raster js` give identical region scores for e2.
